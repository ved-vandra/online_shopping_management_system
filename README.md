<p>The Online Shopping Management System is a web-based application designed to facilitate the buying and selling of products over the internet. It provides a platform where users can browse a wide range of products, add items to a shopping cart, and securely complete purchases using various payment options. The system also includes features for managing product listings, inventory, customer information, and order processing. This project aims to simplify the shopping experience for customers while providing an efficient backend management tool for sellers and administrators.</p>

<h2>Key Features</h2>

<h4>Customer Module</h4>

<li>User registration and login</li>
<li>Product search and filtering</li>
<li>Product details view</li>
<li>Add to cart and wishlist</li>
<li>Checkout and order tracking</li>
<li>Secure online payment</li>
<li>Order history and invoice download</li>

<h4>Admin Module</h4>

<li>Admin login and dashboard</li>
<li>Add/edit/delete product listings</li>
<li>Manage categories and inventory</li>
<li>View customer orders and update order status</li>
<li>User management (view and manage users)</li>
<li>Sales and transaction reports</li>

<h4>General Features</h4>

<li>Responsive and user-friendly interface</li>
<li>Email notifications for order confirmation and status updates</li>
<li>Search engine and product recommendations</li>
<li>Review and rating system</li>
<li>Data encryption and security best practices</li>
